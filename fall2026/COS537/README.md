# Course Materials

This repository contains instructional materials, examples, labs, projects, and other resources developed and organized for **[Artificial Intelligence / COS 537]**.

The course introduces students to modern artificial intelligence concepts through a combination of foundational topics, hands-on activities, programming exercises, and practical projects. Course materials may be updated periodically to reflect developments in the rapidly evolving field of artificial intelligence.

## Course Materials

Materials in this repository may include:

* Lecture notes and slides
* Hands-on labs and exercises
* Programming examples
* Generative AI activities
* Agentic AI activities
* Projects and challenges
* Supplementary learning resources

Most materials have been developed, organized, adapted, or supplemented by the instructor for educational use in this course.

## AWS Machine Learning University Materials

Parts of this course incorporate selected learning materials from **AWS Machine Learning University (AWS MLU)**:

https://github.com/aws-mlu

This includes selected materials from the AWS MLU **Generative AI** and **Agentic AI** educational resources.

Selected AWS MLU materials have been adapted, modified, and supplemented with instructor-developed content to support the learning objectives and hands-on activities of this course.
